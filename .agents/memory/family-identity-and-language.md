---
name: Family identity and language
description: Family-role labels are personal identity, not permission; care language should not feel like work scheduling
---

Let people identify as Mother, Father, Parent, Partner, Grandparent, Helper, or in their own words. Keep that identity independent from administrator/caregiver/helper access.

**Why:** The user explicitly found it unclear who was mother, father or helper. A family label must not imply clinical facts, gender, legal guardianship, or broader access to another adult's private information.

**How to apply:** Do not infer real users' family roles from their name, stage, avatar, or permission level. Offer voluntary profile customization and show access separately. Synthetic demo people may have explicitly authored example identities.

Use pleasant user-facing wording such as “Care time” rather than “shift.”

**Why:** The user asked to replace “shift” with something more pleasant.

**How to apply:** Keep compatibility identifiers unchanged when changing copy; preserve protected-rest and explicit-acceptance behavior.
