The supplied Parenthood MVP build brief and the user's subsequent product direction are the product source of truth. All three stages and every approved feature area are intentional; do not silently cut them into a roadmap. The user renamed the product Kinwell; retain compatibility identifiers rather than migrating data to rename it.

**Why:** The user's supplied brief explicitly requires focused working versions of every included area and distinguishes prototype readiness from a public health-related launch.

**How to apply:** Keep private adult reproductive/wellbeing data separate from shared baby care. Support single parents without requiring a partner. Preserve history through user-confirmed transitions. Never claim calendar synchronization, clinical review, notification delivery, or emergency response that has not happened. Credential-unavailable manual scheduling/calendar export is allowed, but is not completed two-way synchronization.

Kinwell is as much reassurance and anticipatory guidance as coordination: “First time parents not knowing what might be happening in the future/ what might be happening now/ what to plan for/ and prepare for that/ coordinate.”

**Why:** The user explicitly said the app felt primarily like a coordination app and asked to make stage-relevant, expandable “what might be happening” cards the priority.

**How to apply:** Put understandable, non-alarmist guidance before task assignment. Keep plans optional and variable development clear. Make major baby features discoverable without many horizontal tabs; never hide saved history because age changes.

Period tracking must remain available at every stage and let its author choose visibility; Flo export import is also requested.

**Why:** The user could not find periods outside preparation and could not tell whether another person could see them.

**How to apply:** Private by default, explicit author consent and named read-only recipients, plain per-person sharing labels, revocable server access. Other private adult records do not become shared by this exception.
